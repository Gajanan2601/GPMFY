About Mumbai

Mumbai is the capital city of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. It is famous for Bollywood, the Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Juhu Beach, and its fast-paced lifestyle. Mumbai is one of the most populated cities in the world and is known as the "City of Dreams."

Famous Tourist Places

  • Gateway of India
  • Marine Drive
  • Juhu Beach
  • Elephanta Caves
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST)

Famous Foods

  2. Pav Bhaji
  3. Bhel Puri
  4. Misal Pav
  5. Bombay Sandwich
