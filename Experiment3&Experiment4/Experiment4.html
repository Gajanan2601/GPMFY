Mumbai - The City of Dreams

About Mumbai

Mumbai is the capital city of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. It is famous for Bollywood, the Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Juhu Beach and its vibrant culture. Mumbai is known as the "City of Dreams" because millions of people come here to fulfill their dreams.

Famous Tourist Places

  • Gateway of India
  • Marine Drive
  • Juhu Beach
  • Elephanta Caves
  • Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST)

Famous Foods

  2. Pav Bhaji
  3. Bhel Puri
  4. Misal Pav
  5. Bombay Sandwich
