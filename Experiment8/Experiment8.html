Autonomous Institute

Government Polytechnic Mumbai

Established in 1957, G.P. Mumbai is an autonomous institute of the Government of Maharashtra, accredited by NBA and recognized by AICTE.

  • Located on a prime 28-acre Bandra (E) campus
  • Diploma programs under Autonomous Status
  • Equipped with high-tech research & testing labs
  • Operates under DTE Maharashtra guidance
