About Government Polytechnic Mumbai

Government Polytechnic Mumbai (GPM) is one of Maharashtra's oldest and most prestigious autonomous diploma engineering institutes, established in 1960. The institute provides quality technical education with modern laboratories, experienced faculty, industry-oriented curriculum, excellent placement opportunities, and a student-friendly campus.
