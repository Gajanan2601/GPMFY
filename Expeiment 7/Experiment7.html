Government Polytechnic Mumbai

Established in 1957, Government Polytechnic Mumbai is a premier autonomous institute managed by the Government of Maharashtra and approved by AICTE.

Situated on a 28-acre campus in Bandra (East), the college offers NBA-accredited diploma engineering courses with modern laboratory and research facilities.

Engineering Departments

The institute provides 3-year full-time diploma courses in the following branches:

  • Computer Engineering
  • Information Technology
  • Civil Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Electronics Engineering

Training & Placement Cell

GP Mumbai maintains strong ties with industry leaders to facilitate student internships and campus recruitments.

  • Placement Rate: Over 85% eligible students placed annually
  • Highest Salary Package: Up to 12 LPA
  • Top Recruiters: Tata Power, L&T, Siemens, Reliance Industries, TCS
